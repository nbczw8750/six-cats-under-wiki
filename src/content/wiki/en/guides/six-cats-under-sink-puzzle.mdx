This is the puzzle that stops most runs: the kitchen sink only fills when four small actions land in the right sequence, and one missing TV channel silently disables everything else. The fix below is the exact click order, plus what each piece is for.

## What do you need before touching the sink?

You need the TV remote first — without it there is no way to change channels, and the channel change is the trigger. The remote comes out of the sofa in the living room, and everything else you need is already sitting in the kitchen waiting to be clicked.

| Piece you need | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| TV remote | Spoc claws it out of the sofa when the shelf fan blows the houseplant |
| TV turned on | Pick up the remote (it looks like a small wooden block) and switch the TV on |
| Pink mouse toy | Squeaks when clicked — it lives on the kitchen counter |
| Bird towel | Hangs beside the sink; Geoffrey brushes it in |

## What is the exact click order?

<Callout type="warn" title="The lion channel cannot be skipped">This is where the stall happens: without the lion roaring channel on the TV, the pink mouse does nothing no matter how many times you click it. Run the five actions in order.</Callout>

1. Click the pink mouse toy on the counter — it squeaks and frightens Geoffrey.

![Click the pink mouse toy on the counter](/images/articles/点击台面上粉色老鼠玩具.webp)

2. Click the hanging bird towel so Geoffrey brushes it into the sink.

![Click the hanging bird towel](/images/articles/点击挂着的鸟毛巾.webp)

3. Use the remote to flip the TV to the lion roaring channel.

![Switch the TV to the lion roaring channel](/images/articles/切换电视频道%20→%20狮子吼叫频道.webp)

4. Click the pink mouse toy a second time.

![Click the pink mouse toy again](/images/articles/再次点老鼠玩具.webp)

5. Open the faucet and fill the sink with water.

![Open the faucet and fill the sink with water](/images/articles/打开水龙头.webp)

## Why does the pink mouse toy do nothing?

Because a condition is missing, not because the toy is broken. The squeak alone only startles Geoffrey — it takes the lion channel on TV, the towel lying in the sink and the water in the right stage to walk him up onto the kitchen counter. Re-check the TV screen first: if it is still showing the cake baking channel, flip it and repeat from step 4.

## What happens after the sink fills?

Geoffrey climbs onto the counter right beside the fish tank — the exact position the next puzzle needs. From there the chain runs straight into [unlocking the fish food and feeding the goldfish](/guides/six-cats-under-goldfish-puzzle/). If you are following the run page by page, you are now at [step 5 of the walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/), and the only thing left afterwards is [getting Frederick off the fridge](/guides/six-cats-under-frederick-puzzle/).
