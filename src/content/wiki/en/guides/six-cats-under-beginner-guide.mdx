Six Cats Under is built for one sitting: a pixel-art apartment, six cats and a ghost who wants them safely outside. This page gets you from a blank screen to your first working interactions — the full ordered run lives in the [walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/).

## How do you actually play it?

Everything happens through the mouse: you click objects in the rooms and watch which cat reacts. You never control a cat directly, there is no inventory to manage and no keyboard controls to learn — if an object reacts to the cursor, it can be clicked. The apartment is one connected puzzle board, so an action you trigger in the bathroom can move a cat you need in the kitchen.

| First-session fact | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Controls | Mouse click only — no keyboard shortcuts, no menus |
| Who you play | The grandmother's ghost, not one of the cats |
| Typical run | Two to three minutes once the chain clicks |
| Fail states | None — no death, no timer, unlimited retries |
| Win condition | All six cats walk out the front door |

## What should I click first?

Start with the two moves that unlock half the apartment:

1. Click the wall clock so it rings — the black cat Geoffrey wakes up, and he is the engine of almost every puzzle that follows.

![Click the wall clock to wake the black cat Geoffrey](/images/articles/点击墙上时钟.webp)

2. Flush the toilet whenever a cat sits in the wrong spot — the sound pulls Geoffrey across the room, and you can repeat it as often as you like.

![Flush the toilet to lure the black cat Geoffrey](/images/articles/点击马桶冲水.webp)

From there the chain keeps building — window, lamp, shelf fan, TV remote — and the [full walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/) carries it step by step, with a screenshot for every click.

<Callout type="tip" title="There is no wrong click">No interaction is one-shot. If a click seems to do nothing, work another part of the room and come back — the toilet flush and the shelf fan are reusable tools you will use twice each.</Callout>

## Can you get permanently stuck?

No. Six Cats Under has no death mechanic and no time limit, so a half-finished attempt just leaves every object waiting to be clicked again. The game tracks a single progress bar: when the sixth cat crosses the threshold, the run ends. If you want a preview of how much work each cat actually needs, the [difficulty ranking](/guides/six-cats-under-cats-ranked/) shows which three cats carry puzzles and which three simply walk out.
