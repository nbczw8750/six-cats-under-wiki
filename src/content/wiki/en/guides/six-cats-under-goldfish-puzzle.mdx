The goldfish is the hinge of the whole run: nothing Frederick-related happens until this fish is frying in the pan. Three gates stand between you and that smell — unlock the food, feed at the right moment, then heat the stove.

## How do you unlock the fish food?

The fish-food bag on the kitchen counter starts locked. Click the heart ornament hanging from the ceiling — it falls, hits the bag and the food becomes clickable.

![Click the heart ornament so it falls onto the fish-food bag](/images/articles/点击上方悬挂的爱心装饰.webp)

That only makes the food available. Whether feeding works right now depends on where Geoffrey is standing, which is the question that trips up most players.

## Why will not the goldfish drop into the pan?

Because Geoffrey has to be beside the fish tank when you feed him — he reacts to the food, and his reaction is what sends the goldfish from the tank into the pan on the stove. If he is still wandering the living room, the stove does nothing.

| Symptom | What is actually wrong | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clicking the fish food does nothing useful | The bag is still locked | Drop the heart ornament onto it first |
| Fish gets fed but the pan stays empty | Geoffrey is not beside the tank | Finish the sink puzzle so he climbs onto the counter, then feed again |
| Pan still empty after feeding | The stove was not opened | Open the stove so the pan heats — the fish drops in |

## What is the full chain in order?

1. Do the sink puzzle so Geoffrey climbs onto the counter beside the tank (see the [sink puzzle solution](/guides/six-cats-under-sink-puzzle/) for the exact five clicks).
2. Click the heart ornament to unlock the fish food.
3. Click the fish food while Geoffrey stands beside the tank.

![Feed the goldfish while Geoffrey stands beside the tank](/images/articles/点击鱼食.webp)

4. Open the stove and heat the pan — the goldfish drops in and the smell starts spreading through the kitchen.

![Open the stove and heat the pan](/images/articles/点开炉灶.webp)

The smell itself does not wake anybody yet: it is bait for [the cat on top of the fridge](/guides/six-cats-under-frederick-puzzle/). This whole chain sits at steps 6 and 7 of the [full walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/), and the stove must be opened before you open the front door.
