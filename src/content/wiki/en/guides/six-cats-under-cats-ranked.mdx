Six Cats Under hands you six cats but only three real jobs. This ranking counts one thing: how many puzzle steps stand between a cat and the front door, taken straight from the [full walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/).

## Which cat is the hardest to get out?

Frederick — and it is not close. Every other puzzle in the apartment exists to put a pan of fried fish under his nose: wake the black cat, spill the yarn, dig out the remote, run the sink puzzle, feed the goldfish, heat the stove, aim the fan. Nothing about him can be skipped or shortcut.

| Rank | Cat | What stands between it and the door | Load |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Frederick | The entire fried-fish chain ends at the fridge top | Heavy |
| 2 | Geoffrey | Woken, frightened and repositioned throughout the run | Heavy (passive) |
| 3 | Spoc | One trigger: the fan blows the plant, he claws the sofa | One move |
| 4 (tie) | Pumpkin | Nothing — it walks out when the door opens | None |
| 4 (tie) | Baroness | Nothing — same as every bystander cat | None |
| 4 (tie) | Mr.Spock | Nothing — stays on the bathroom door frame | None |

## Why is Geoffrey ranked second if he does everything?

Because he is a tool, not an obstacle: you ring the clock to wake him, flush the toilet to drag him around, squeak the mouse to scare him onto the counter. He touches more puzzles than any other cat, but none of them are *his* — he never has to be convinced twice for the same reason.

## Do the other three cats do anything at all?

No. Pumpkin stays on the living room bookshelf, Baroness sits on the bedroom bed, Mr.Spock lies on the bathroom door frame — and all three walk out once the door is open and [Frederick has jumped down](/guides/six-cats-under-frederick-puzzle/). They are the reward for finishing the chain, not part of it.

If you only remember one line: hardest cat first ([Frederick](/guides/six-cats-under-frederick-puzzle/)), hardest stall in the middle ([the sink puzzle](/guides/six-cats-under-sink-puzzle/)), and everything else is clicks — the [beginner guide](/guides/six-cats-under-beginner-guide/) covers the rest.
