The door can already be open and the game still is not over, because one cat refuses to move: Frederick, asleep on top of the fridge. He does not respond to clicks, lures or noise — only the smell of fried fish, delivered by fan.

## How do you get Frederick off the fridge?

You need two things in place: a frying pan with a fish in it, and the shelf fan pointed at the top of the fridge. The pan comes from [the goldfish chain](/guides/six-cats-under-goldfish-puzzle/); the fan is the same one you used to dig the TV remote out of the sofa.

1. Open the stove and heat the pan — the goldfish drops in, fries, and the smell starts spreading through the kitchen.

![Open the stove so the goldfish fries](/images/articles/点开炉灶.webp)

2. Adjust the shelf fan so it aims at the top of the fridge, then switch it on — the breeze carries the smell upward.

![Aim the fan at the top of the fridge and switch it on](/images/articles/再次调整风扇方向.webp)

3. Frederick smells it, jumps down from the fridge, and with him in the group all six cats walk out the front door — the run ends.

<Callout type="warn" title="The door is not the finish line">The front door unlocks as early as the first toilet flush, long before anything here is done. Open it whenever you like — nothing ends and nothing is lost — but the run only completes once Frederick is down and the sixth cat crosses the threshold.</Callout>

## Why does clicking Frederick do nothing?

Because he is only scripted to react to one stimulus: the fried-fish smell reaching the top of the fridge. Every other interaction in the apartment — the clock, the toilet, the mouse toy — moves other cats. If the fan is running but nothing happens, the pan has not been heated yet: go back and open the stove.

## Where do I find that fan again?

Wherever you left it after the TV remote step — many players leave it in the living room pointed at the houseplant. It never disappears; you only re-aim it. This closing chain is [step 7 of the walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/), and it is the last thing between you and a finished run — the only earlier stall point is the [sink puzzle](/guides/six-cats-under-sink-puzzle/).
