Short game, common questions. This page answers the ones that come up before and during a first run — for the step-by-step solution itself, the [walkthrough](/guides/six-cats-under-walkthrough/) is the page you want.

## What kind of game is Six Cats Under?

It is a short, pixel-art point-and-click puzzle that runs in the browser on itch.io. You play the ghost of a grandmother who passed away unexpectedly, trapped in her own apartment with one wish: get her six cats safely outside. There are no levels, no menus to manage and no score — the apartment itself is the whole game board.

## How long does a run take?

Two to three minutes once the chain makes sense, and your first blind run takes only slightly longer because the game is small on purpose. It tracks exactly one progress bar from the moment you start: six cats in, zero cats left means finished. That length is also why [the difficulty ranking](/guides/six-cats-under-cats-ranked/) is measured in puzzle steps rather than hours.

## Do you control the cats directly?

No — and that is the whole trick. You click objects (the wall clock, the toilet, the lamp, the TV remote, the fan) and each object provokes a specific cat into moving. Learn which cat reacts to what and the run plays itself: the [beginner guide](/guides/six-cats-under-beginner-guide/) lists the two opening moves, and the [sink puzzle](/guides/six-cats-under-sink-puzzle/) is the first real coordination test.

## What should I do if I am stuck?

<Callout type="tip" title="Two tools carry half the game">The toilet flush pulls the black cat wherever you need him, and the shelf fan is reused from the sofa puzzle all the way to the final smell. If something is not reacting, these two are usually the missing link.</Callout>

There is no state you cannot undo: no death, no timer, no consumables. If a click does nothing, click something else and come back later — most stalls are one missing TV channel or one unheated pan away from resolving. The two known stall points have dedicated pages: the [sink puzzle](/guides/six-cats-under-sink-puzzle/) and [Frederick on the fridge](/guides/six-cats-under-frederick-puzzle/).
